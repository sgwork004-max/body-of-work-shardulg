KOEL Research

A two-and-a-half-month qualitative research engagement for Kirloskar Oil Engines: map the customer journey for the electric pumps segment, find the insight-shaped gaps, and build a go-to-market strategy. It began by winning the internal bid to lead the project and ended in a boardroom – seventy-three conversations later, presented to the Managing Director and senior leadership.

01 · the fieldwork

Farmers, retailers, plumbers, and one aggressive interviewee.

Built the research questionnaire and iterated it three-plus times, then took it into the field: self-initiated interviews with farmers, retailers, and channel partners across Maharashtra. Every one of the seventy-three interviews was manually transcribed and analysed. One interviewee turned aggressive mid-conversation; the interview continued, and so did the transcript. Fieldwork is not a metaphor.

02 · the synthesis

A thousand data points, one prioritised story.

Clustered 1,000+ data points using the 4Ps framework on Miro boards, co-synthesised insights with an external consultant, and prioritised by impact against feasibility. Every insight left the room in the same format – and the format is the transferable asset:

context→observation→insight→data→action

Leadership communication ran on curiosity-based prompts – "this made us wonder", "how might we" – because curiosity builds engagement where assertion builds defensiveness.

03 · the boardroom

Presented to the Managing Director.

After a mid-project review with the VP of Product and an external content review of the final deck, the findings were presented to KOEL's Managing Director and senior leadership team. The recommendations moved into active discussion on retailer engagement and communication strategy. A workshop-style visual deck landed where a linear slide march would not have.

moments that mattered

What mattered.

  • late nightsField visits that ran past dark – the point where "on-ground" stopped being a phrase and became a schedule.
  • the circleA listening circle with teammates to process weeks of field experience. Research is done by people; the people need maintenance too.
  • the finishFinal presentation, then cake. An intense journey deserves a proper full stop.

field note

What changed my judgment.

Ground-up qualitative research at corporate scale: how to keep empathy in the conversation and still walk out with usable data, how to map insights to specific business levers instead of reporting observations, and how to hold research integrity while speaking corporate language. The insight format built here has been reused in every research-shaped project since.
