## Platform

web

## Users

The primary readers are recruiters, founders, CEOs, and commercially minded hiring managers evaluating Shardul Gupta for operator roles, founder-facing work, or paid projects. They need to understand who he is, how he thinks, what he can own, and what evidence supports those conclusions.

## Product Purpose

This site presents Shardul as an early-career commercial operator whose education has been built through direct execution. It should let a reader quickly understand his trajectory across buyer-facing sales, field research, outreach systems, founder's office work, independent tools, and public writing, then go deeper into the work that supports that positioning.

Success means a serious reader can identify Shardul's operating strengths, see credible proof of implementation and outcomes, and know how to start a relevant conversation.

## Positioning

This is not a generic student portfolio or a gallery of finished artifacts. It shows how commercial judgment was built across real operating environments, moving from direct selling and fieldwork toward systems, positioning, and ownership.

Shardul works where positioning, buyer behaviour, and execution meet. His strength is turning unclear commercial problems into clearer messages, decision-ready insight, or operating systems a team can use.

## Operating Context

The work spans founder-led companies, a premium retail sales floor, field research across five cities, B2B outreach, product and brand messaging, delivery visibility, hiring systems, independent macOS tools, a file-native second brain, and long-form essays.

Readers move from a cinematic operating-journey hero into a concise profile, selected work experience, projects, independent systems, writing, and contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Brand positioning, website strategy, product narratives, and commercial messaging
- Buyer research, objection diagnosis, prospect qualification, and message testing
- Founder-facing execution across operations, delivery visibility, hiring, and product go-to-market
- B2B outreach systems and handover-ready operating processes
- Independent software and knowledge-system building
- Static web implementation with responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- All numerical claims, role descriptions, dates, company names, and project facts must remain grounded in the existing source content
- No testimonials, clients, benchmarks, or outcomes may be invented

## Brand Commitments

- Existing name: Shardul Gupta, represented by the SG/ mark
- Voice: commercially sharp, direct, analytical, confident, and practical
- Avoid student-energy positioning, generic marketing language, corporate buzzwords, and hype
- Preserve the established black and white editorial identity with restrained yellow highlighting
- Preserve the hero's impossible operating landscape as the site's primary visual signature
- The work itself should remain the central evidence, while the profile makes the person and operating thesis legible

## Product Principles

1. Lead with commercial judgment, then prove it with specific work.
2. Make Shardul's operating identity legible before asking readers to interpret the archive.
3. Prefer evidence, decisions, and implementation over abstract capability claims.
4. Reward both scanning and depth: the homepage should orient quickly, while detail routes carry the full journey.
5. Preserve an honest early-career trajectory without reducing the work to student or intern framing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should remain usable with keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings and landmarks, useful image alternatives, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts that preserve the complete story on smaller screens.
