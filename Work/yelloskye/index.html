YelloSKYE

Built the commercial operating layer around a drone survey and reality-capture company. Website, positioning, brand, delivery visibility, product GTM, and hiring moved in parallel. The role was not about supporting one function. It was about making the company easier to understand, sell, and run.

01 · positioning

One commercial story across every surface.

Built YelloSKYE's brand messaging system from founder inputs, customer insight, and market research. Turned that thinking into a usable structure across the website, LinkedIn, sales collateral, and outreach, so the company stopped sounding different every time the medium changed.

02 · website

Built from a blank sitemap to a live commercial surface.

Owned the website from scratch: sitemap, positioning, page architecture, copy, design direction, and launch iterations. The site was shaped for inbound discovery and strengthened for SEO and GEO visibility, turning it from a digital placeholder into the clearest expression of what the company does and why it matters.

03 · delivery operations

Made delivery visible before it became urgent.

Built and automated an operations MIS that tracks pipeline, work in progress, delays, SLA health, bottlenecks, and weekly forecasts across delivery teams. The system created a shared operating view for the founder and execution teams, replacing fragmented updates with a clear picture of what was moving, what was stuck, and what required intervention.

pipeline→WIP→SLA health→forecast

04 · product gtm

Turned platform capability into a sales story.

Supported platform GTM through product messaging, use-case narratives, sales collateral, and dashboard-led positioning. The work translated a technical product into commercial language buyers could understand, with each use case tied to an operating problem rather than a feature list.

05 · hiring

Built the filter before making the hire.

Ran hiring across three roles, screening more than 150 candidates and building evaluation frameworks for consistent comparison. The process moved from volume to signal and led to three final hires.

how the work connected

Five systems, one commercial direction.

  • PositioningEstablished the commercial language used by the website, LinkedIn, sales collateral, and outreach.
  • WebsiteTurned the positioning into a live discovery surface built for clarity, search visibility, and iteration.
  • OperationsMade delivery performance visible through one automated MIS spanning pipeline to forecast.
  • Product GTMTranslated the platform into buyer-facing narratives grounded in use cases and dashboards.
  • HiringBuilt consistent evaluation into the process and closed three roles from a pool of 150+ candidates.

operating principle

The work compounds when the systems connect.

The website was stronger because the positioning was clear. Sales collateral improved because the product story was sharper. Delivery conversations changed because the MIS made performance visible. The role taught me to treat brand, revenue, operations, product, and talent as one connected commercial system.
